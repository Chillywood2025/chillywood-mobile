# Merch Risk Review

Status: draft operations rules. Production merch is not active.

Review before production merch:

- product description accuracy;
- prohibited/unsafe/infringing merch;
- inventory or fulfillment availability;
- suspicious order velocity;
- shipping-address anomalies;
- repeated failed payments;
- refund/return abuse;
- chargeback/dispute history;
- creator account risk;
- customer account risk;
- fulfillment proof and tracking availability;
- support escalation history.

Actions may include blocking a product, canceling an order, holding fulfillment, refunding, requesting more evidence, disabling creator merch, or escalating to Owner/Admin.
