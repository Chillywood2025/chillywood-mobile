# Money Production Approval Gates

Status: Owner/Admin readiness gates. These gates do not activate production money.

## Digital Production Gate

Requires:

- Google Play / RevenueCat production products configured;
- Play review passed;
- Data Safety reviewed;
- privacy policy reviewed;
- refund policy reviewed;
- support workflow ready;
- fraud/risk rules ready;
- production product tier mapping approved;
- Owner approval.

## Merch Production Gate

Requires:

- Stripe live account ready;
- physical-goods-only checkout verified;
- fulfillment plan ready;
- refund/return policy ready;
- shipping policy ready;
- tax/sales tax review ready;
- support workflow ready;
- fraud/order review ready;
- Data Safety reviewed for shipping/contact data;
- Owner approval.

## Payout Production Gate

Requires:

- Stripe Connect live ready;
- KYC/tax requirements ready;
- payout terms accepted;
- tax reporting workflow ready;
- fraud/risk holds ready;
- chargeback reserve ready;
- support workflow ready;
- negative balance plan ready;
- Owner approval.

No gate can be completed by a screenshot alone. Each gate needs attached evidence, approver, timestamp, rollback plan, and immutable audit.
