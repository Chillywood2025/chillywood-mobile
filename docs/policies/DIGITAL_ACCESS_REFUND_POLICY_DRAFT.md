# Digital Access Refund Policy Draft

Status: draft only. Production digital sales are not active.

Covered products:

- Premium through Google Play / RevenueCat;
- paid content access;
- Watch-Party Live tickets;
- Live access passes;
- Live seat passes;
- event passes;
- creator tips/support.

Rules to review:

- Google Play refund handling and app-support escalation;
- access revoked after refund/reversal where backed;
- no guaranteed access if content/room/event is unsafe, blocked, private, draft, deleted, admin-removed, malware, canceled, ended, stale, removed, or disabled;
- ticket/access/seat refunds do not grant publish/host/speaker/mod/admin authority;
- tips do not unlock access and may have different refund posture;
- abusive refund behavior may trigger risk review;
- chargebacks may trigger access revocation and creator holds.
