# Refund, Cancellation, And Return Policy Draft

Status: draft only. Not legal advice. Production money and production merch are not active.

This umbrella policy links digital access refunds, merch returns/refunds, cancellations, reversals, and chargebacks. Final policy requires legal, support, provider, tax, and Owner/Admin review.

Principles:

- refund decisions depend on product type, provider rules, safety state, fulfillment state, and abuse/risk review;
- refunds or reversals may revoke access where backed;
- safety removal does not automatically guarantee access or refund;
- chargebacks may trigger access revocation, creator holds, and risk review;
- support must not promise guaranteed refunds.
