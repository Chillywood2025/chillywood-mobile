## Material correction: Pennsylvania reporting election is suspended

The first matrix incorrectly treated the historical $10,000 election/reporting provisions as potentially active merely because they remain printed in the consolidated code. **Do not implement or tell Robert that Pennsylvania currently imposes that $10,000 election.** Pennsylvania Act13-2019 suspended §§213–213.6 from July1 2019. The Department's updated bulletin explicitly calls out that suspension. The suspension has a statutory contingent lapse mechanism, so monitor enacted changes/notices rather than treating dormant text as operative.

Primary sources: [Act13-2019](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/WU01/LI/LI/US/HTM/2019/0/0013..HTM); [DOR July2019 tax summary](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/revenue/documents/taxlawpoliciesbulletinsnotices/taxsummaries/documents/2019_tax_summary_jul.pdf); [updated Bulletin2019-01 cover notice](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/revenue/documents/taxlawpoliciesbulletinsnotices/taxbulletins/sut/documents/st_bulletin_2019-01.pdf).

For current administration, DOR's [online retailer page](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/resources/tax-types-and-information/sales-use-and-hotel-occupancy-tax/online-retailers) measures the threshold by calendar year and directs vendors exceeding$100,000 in the prior year to register. The statute §201(b)(3.5) says at least$100,000 during the preceding twelve-month calendar period. Own+facilitated sales count. **Precise equality and statutory/administrative period remain reconciliation questions**, not reasons to revive the suspended election. Build a pre-threshold review alert, not an unsupported exact-edge production rule.

# 2026 information-reporting conflicts: independent verification

Research date: 14 November 2026. This addendum supplements, rather than silently edits, the original report. These are filing-rule research findings, not a determination that Chi’llywood is the obligated payer. Allocate that responsibility by contract and actual settlement flow first. Reporting thresholds are not exemptions from income tax.

## Illinois: current transmission route confirmed

[IDOR Publication110, revised October2026](https://tax.illinois.gov/research/publications/pubs/filing-and-storage-requirements-for-employers-and-payers.html) requires Illinois-address 1099-K electronic submission when federal electronic filing is required or when at least four transactions cumulatively exceed $1,000. Its tax-year2026 deadline is March31,2027. The route is Illinois IRIS Electronic Transmission, not MyTaxIllinois. Existing transmitters must follow the September2026 re-registration change and IDR987/IRS IRIS-format instructions. A receipt alone is not acceptance: obtain the subsequent accepted status. Other 1099 forms are generally not required unless requested; voluntary withholding reporting is distinct. This resolves the earlier uncertainty about whether Illinois IRIS was an actual state system.

## Virginia: statutory synthesis supports $2,000 for 2026

[Virginia58.1-356](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/chapter3/section58.1-356/) substitutes IRC6041(a)'s threshold for the federal TPSO de minimis and addresses Virginia-address payees. [2026 conformity statute58.1-301](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacodeupdates/title58.1/section58.1-301/) adopts the IRC as of December31,2025 and lists exceptions that do not include that threshold. The federal2026 threshold is $2,000. Therefore the statutory synthesis supports $2,000 for2026; the old2020 $600 explanation should not be treated as current. The statute provides electronic reporting within30days of relevant federal deadlines. Validate the annual2026 submission specifications and exact form deadline before filing; this is a legal-text inference, not a newly issued agency filing determination.

## Vermont: current agency amount corroborated

[Vermont agency1099-K page](https://tax.vermont.gov/business-and-corp/withholding-tax/1099-k) currently states $2,000 or more (search-indexed agency text; direct page access was restricted). [32VSA5862d](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/32/151/05862d) substitutes6041(a) for the TPSO de minimis and requires copies for Vermont-address payees within30days of IRS deadlines. [Enacted Act164 of2026, section60](https://legislature.vermont.gov/Documents/2026/Docs/ACTS/ACT164/ACT164%20As%20Enacted.pdf) updates5824's federal conformity date to December31,2025. This corroborates the current amount; do not carry forward the old$600 threshold as the2026 answer. Exact2026 electronic-format and annual deadline implementation remains to be verified before submission.

## Maryland: statutory amount and unusual deadline need operational reconciliation

[Tax-General10-825](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtg&section=10-825) expressly uses the6041(a) threshold for qualifying payees required to file Maryland returns/declarations. [Tax-General1-101(k)](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtg&section=1-101) defines IRC as Title26 without a fixed date. Together these support a2026 $2,000 reading, but annual2026 reporting guidance and any applicable decoupling require reconciliation before production filing. Critically,10-825 says at least30days BEFORE federal deadlines, not after. Do not compute a calendar from generic federal dates without reviewing current state instructions. This remains an operational/legal confirmation item, not cleared.

## Control implication

Maintain effective-year rules per form and state, with explicit >= versus > and transaction conditions. Preserve evidence version, responsible filer, recipient state/source rules, transmission route, acceptance acknowledgement and corrections. Do not use one federal threshold nationwide and do not file both1099-K and1099-NEC for the same reportable settlement just because a provider emitted one form.

## Rhode Island and Wisconsin: opposite 2026 changes

Rhode Island's February2026 $100 advisory is not the final current-law answer. The legislature's [June30,2026 enactment announcement](https://www.rilegislature.gov/pressrelease/_layouts/15/ril.pressrelease.inputform/DisplayForm.aspx?ID=376684&List=c8baae31-3c10-431c-8dcd-9dbbe21ce3e9) identifies H8604A/S3367A aligning information reporting with federal rules. Consult the expanded inventory's enacted-text analysis; do not apply the old advisory universally or assume every form has a$2,000 threshold.

Wisconsin's [October2026 Withholding Tax Update](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/WithholdingTaxUpdate/26-1.pdf) explicitly states it has not adopted the federal increase from$600 to$2,000 for the relevant payments. Therefore federal conformity must be tested separately for each state/form, not presumed. Form1099-K withholding requirements and other forms' requirements remain distinct.

## What this covers
1099-K, 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC reporting by the legally responsible payer.
