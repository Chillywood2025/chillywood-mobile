# Quorum, Veto, and Dissent

Risk-based quorum is enforced by closed role and vote types. A person/model identity
and a council role can vote only once per decision version. First-round assessments
are blind. Recused members do not count.

Minimum quorum increases with risk. Support must exceed opposition and the four
required critics must participate. An unresolved mandatory veto for security,
privacy, auth/RLS, money, user rights, public release, legal, or retention scope
always defeats a majority.

Dissent is immutable and visible in the decision manifest. A dissent records the
role, reason hash, evidence hashes, predicted risk, conditions under which it
matters, and whether it is resolved or accepted as explicit residual risk. It
cannot be hidden or deleted to manufacture quorum.
