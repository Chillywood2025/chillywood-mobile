## Support Steps

- check Stripe Connect onboarding/KYC/tax/provider status where safe;
- check holds, fraud notes, chargebacks, refund reserve, and policy status where backed;
- escalate tax/legal questions to tax/legal review;
- escalate payout activation questions to Owner/Admin.
