# Refund Support Playbook

Status: draft support playbook. Production money is not active.

## User-Facing Template

Thanks for reaching out. We can review the purchase/access record and explain the next step. Some digital purchases may be handled through Google Play, and access can still be limited by safety, moderation, room, event, or content availability rules.

## Support Steps

- identify product type and source;
- inspect provider event, purchase intent, access grant, and ledger/order readout where allowed;
- check content/room/event safety state;
- check refund/reversal/chargeback status;
- escalate to Owner/Admin for policy decision;
- document audit reason.

Support cannot guarantee refunds, promise access after unsafe/removed content, or provide legal/tax advice.
