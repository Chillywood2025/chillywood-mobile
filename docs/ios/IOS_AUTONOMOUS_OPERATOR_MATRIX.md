## Component-level parity addendum

The system rows above remain the authority boundary. Their subordinate surfaces are machine-registered in `config/autonomy/autonomous-components.json`: media automation worker and malware scanner under media; heartbeat plus disabled GitHub/Cloudflare scheduler templates under LiveKit; terminal retry under notifications; provider adapters under release/observability; Android Firebase Test Lab plus independent iOS readiness under installed QA; and the transactionally clustered/routed User Report Router under support. Autonomous approval and alert automation are protected control planes, while manual release workflows and administrative imports remain non-autonomous utilities.

The inventory guard scans systemd, scheduled workflows, Cloudflare cron templates, long-running queue workers, database cron, and operator-like functions. The full matrix and lifecycle/readback result is `ALL_PLATFORM_AUTONOMY_PARITY_REPORT.md`.
