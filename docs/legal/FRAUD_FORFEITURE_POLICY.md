# Fraud And Forfeiture Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

> Repo launch note: Attorney review required before public launch. Enforcement, forfeiture, and payout-hold language must be approved before use.

Chi'llywood protects users, creators, rights holders, payment providers, sponsors, and the platform from fraud and abuse.

## Banned Fraud And Manipulation

Do not engage in:

- fake engagement, fake followers, fake views, fake watch time, fake comments, fake likes, or fake sponsor metrics;
- bots, scripts, click farms, traffic exchanges, scraping, or coordinated manipulation;
- stolen accounts, stolen payment methods, account takeovers, credential theft, or payment fraud;
- chargeback abuse, refund abuse, subscription abuse, or repeated purchase/reversal schemes;
- copyright fraud, false takedowns, fake ownership claims, or counter-notice abuse;
- sponsorship fraud, fake reviews, undisclosed paid promotion, fake brand claims, fake invoices, fake performance, or unsafe product scams;
- payout evasion, identity/KYC fraud, tax form fraud, sanctions evasion, fake payout accounts, or attempts to bypass provider holds;
- ban evasion, account cycling, device/account farms, or return after termination without permission;
- phishing, malware, deceptive links, investment scams, fake giveaways, or fake Premium offers.

## Consequences

To the extent allowed by law and payment-provider rules, Chi'llywood may:

- hold earnings, credits, payouts, sponsor payments, or revenue-share records;
- reverse credits or ledger entries;
- withhold payouts;
- delay payout review;
- disable monetization;
- restrict uploads, live access, chat, comments, sponsor tools, or account access;
- close accounts;
- terminate creator privileges;
- preserve evidence;
- share information with providers, law enforcement, or legal process where appropriate;
- pursue recovery or other remedies.

## Evidence And Review

Chi'llywood may use account data, device/app signals, payment data, provider reports, rights-holder notices, support reports, moderation records, traffic patterns, and admin audit logs to review fraud.

Fraud decisions that affect money, payouts, sponsorships, account status, or creator access should be logged with reason, reviewer, timestamp, affected account/content, and available appeal/review path.

## No Live Enforcement Claim

Current repo truth includes fraud foundation/readout/preflight systems, but no complete live fraud enforcement hook is active. Do not claim automated fraud enforcement or payout forfeiture is operational until backed hooks, review, appeal, legal approval, and affected-surface proof exist.
