# Digital Purchase Terms Draft

Status: draft only. Not legal advice. Production digital purchases are not active beyond separately approved Play/Billing states. Attorney/legal review and Google Play policy review are required before public launch.

## Rail

Android in-app digital goods use Google Play / RevenueCat. Stripe must not sell Android Premium, paid content, Watch-Party tickets, Live access passes, Live seat passes, creator tips, or event passes inside the app.

## Covered Products

- Premium
- Creator tips/support
- Watch-Party Live tickets
- Live Watch-Party access passes
- Live Watch-Party seat passes
- Paid content access
- Event passes

## Access Limits

Digital purchases are subject to safety, moderation, blocking, privacy, availability, room state, event state, and content state. Purchases do not bypass private, draft, deleted, admin-removed, malware, blocked, ended, stale, canceled, removed, or disabled states.

Tickets and access passes grant entry/viewing only. Seat passes grant eligibility only. Host approval is still required for mic/camera/publish. Payment never grants LiveKit publish, host, speaker, moderator, admin, or payout authority.

## Refunds

Refund handling may involve Google Play, RevenueCat, and app support. Refunds or reversals may revoke access where backed. Abusive refund behavior may trigger risk review.

## Sandbox

Sandbox purchases are not production money and are not payable creator earnings.
