# Monetization Policy Legal Review Checklist

Status: internal checklist. Not legal advice. No production money, payouts, cash-out, withdrawal, transfer, payable balance, or production merch checkout is active.

Legal review must approve:

- Creator Monetization Terms.
- Creator Payout Terms.
- Merch Terms.
- Digital Purchase Terms.
- Refund/cancellation/return policies.
- Dispute resolution and governing law placeholders.
- Support contact and response commitments.
- Copyright/DMCA interaction with paid access.
- Moderation and safety removal impact on access/refunds.
- Chargeback and reversal language.
- Creator eligibility and termination rights.
- No earnings guarantee language.
- Sandbox/test activity not payable.
- Data Safety and privacy policy updates for shipping, identity, tax, and financial data.
- Google Play digital-goods rail separation.
- Stripe physical-merch and Connect-readiness separation.
- Owner/Admin approval and rollback rules.

Legal signoff must be attached before any future production activation lane.
