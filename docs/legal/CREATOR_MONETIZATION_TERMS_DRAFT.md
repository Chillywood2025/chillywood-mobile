# Creator Monetization Terms Draft

Status: draft only. Not legal advice. Not active for production money. Attorney/legal review is required before public launch. Production payouts, cash-out, withdrawals, transfers, payable balances, and production checkout are not active.

## Scope

This draft describes future creator monetization eligibility and conduct rules for paid content, tickets, access passes, seat passes, event passes, tips, and physical merch readiness. Sandbox activity is test-only and not payable.

## Eligibility

Creators may need:

- account in good standing;
- age and account requirements satisfied;
- verified email/account identity;
- Premium or approved creator status where required;
- no active safety, copyright, fraud, chargeback, or policy hold;
- approved source content, room, event, or merch record;
- accepted current creator terms and monetization policies;
- future production approval if live money is enabled later.

## Creator Responsibilities

Creators are responsible for:

- owning or having rights to uploaded content and merch descriptions;
- accurate titles, descriptions, pricing labels, and availability claims;
- complying with Community Guidelines, copyright policy, moderation rules, and payment-provider rules;
- not monetizing prohibited, unsafe, stolen, misleading, illegal, infringing, malware, private, deleted, admin-removed, or blocked content.

## Platform Rights

Chi'llwood may remove content, disable monetization, revoke access, pause payouts, hold funds, refund users, reverse ledger rows, require additional review, or terminate monetization access for policy, fraud, copyright, safety, provider, support, legal, or tax reasons.

## Earnings And Sandbox Activity

No earnings are guaranteed. Sandbox purchases, setup rows, test orders, route-backed proof fixtures, provider test events, and not-payable ledger rows are not creator earnings and cannot be cashed out.

Production activation is separate and requires explicit Owner/Admin, legal, tax, fraud, support, provider, Data Safety, and rollback approvals.

## Refunds, Reversals, And Chargebacks

Future creator proceeds may be reduced by refunds, chargebacks, reversals, taxes, app-store/provider fees, fraud holds, support adjustments, moderation removals, or policy violations. Refund or chargeback handling may revoke access where backed.

## Suspension And Termination

Monetization may be suspended or terminated for policy violations, content removal, account compromise, identity mismatch, KYC/tax failure, fraud signals, repeated disputes, or legal/provider requirements.
