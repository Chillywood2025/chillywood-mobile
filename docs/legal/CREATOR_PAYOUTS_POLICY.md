# Creator Payouts Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

> Repo launch note: Attorney review required before public launch. This policy must not be treated as payout activation. Live creator payouts are not active in the current repo truth.

## Current Status

Creator payouts are planned/foundation-only unless Chi'llywood explicitly enables them in a proved release. The current repo has read-only/foundation payout surfaces, Stripe Connect test-mode backend functions, preflight gates, admin readouts consolidated in Owner/Admin Money Center, creator-facing test-mode Connect setup/readiness states in Platform Studio Money Center, Owner/Admin Money kill switches, and a remote-applied default-off creator monetization systems foundation for future paid content, products, tips, immutable earnings ledger rows, payout requests, webhook idempotency, and audit logs. Preproduction test proof may create/reuse real Stripe test connected accounts, generate short-lived test onboarding links, and sync non-payable provider readiness, but those test states are not creator earnings or payout approval. It does not activate live payouts, transfers, cash-out, payable balances, withdrawal, checkout, paid status, fake purchases, fake tips, fake orders, fake KYC/tax/1099 readiness, or fake earnings. Premium subscription revenue belongs to Chi'llywood/platform and is not split with creators.

## Eligibility

If payouts become active, eligibility may require:

- account good standing;
- active creator status;
- identity/KYC verification;
- tax forms;
- approved payout account;
- supported country/region;
- payment-provider approval;
- minimum payout threshold;
- no active fraud, copyright, policy, sanctions, chargeback, refund, or account-compromise hold;
- platform review and immutable audit where required.

## Provider Direction And Permissions

Stripe Connect or an equivalent marketplace payout provider is the preferred direction for creator payout onboarding, KYC/tax readiness, connected-account tracking, transfers, payout rails, 1099/tax tooling, sponsor payments, marketplace/network money, paid content payouts, and merch/product payouts. Android creator tips remain Google Play/RevenueCat, not Stripe. Google Play plus RevenueCat remains the Premium subscription entitlement path and must not become the creator payout system.

Current Stripe Connect status is test-mode/sandbox readiness only. A creator may be able to start backend-created Stripe setup from Platform Studio Money Center when `stripe_connect_enabled` allows readiness review, but Chi'llywood must not call money available until provider readiness, KYC, tax/1099 readiness, hold clearance, minimum payout, legal/accounting review, Owner-approved workflow, `payouts_enabled`, and `live_money_enabled` all pass. Owner/Admin Money Center readouts and switches are review/status/control-plane only and cannot release money.

Preproduction test mode can prove blocked states and fee math only. Scheduled payout fee remains `$0`; optional instant cash-out remains `1.5%` with no default cap, shown separately before confirmation if a future live lane is approved. A `$100` instant cash-out preview is `$1.50`, and a `$1,000` instant cash-out preview is `$15.00`. These previews do not create a payable balance, payout request, transfer, bank payout, tax form, or 1099 readiness.

Future Owner/Admin money permissions must be role-controlled and server-side. Owners may configure payout/monetization settings later through backed workflows. Admins may view payout/readiness/status panels and flag payout issues where backed, but must not directly edit creator balances or release money without a backed Owner-approved server-side flow. Money switch changes require a backend RPC, reason, and immutable audit. Moderators have no money access.

## Holds, Reversals, And Forfeiture

To the extent allowed by law and payment-provider rules, Chi'llywood may delay, withhold, reverse, offset, or forfeit payouts or credits for:

- fraud;
- chargebacks, refunds, or payment disputes;
- copyright disputes or takedown claims;
- stolen content;
- fake engagement;
- sponsorship fraud;
- account compromise;
- sanctions or unsupported country;
- illegal conduct;
- prohibited content;
- policy violations;
- provider requirements;
- tax/KYC failure.

## Taxes

Creators are responsible for taxes on income they receive. Chi'llywood may require tax information and may report payments where required by law.

## No Earnings Guarantee

Chi'llywood does not guarantee earnings, payout availability, audience size, sponsor deals, ad revenue, tips, paid-content sales, or revenue-share amounts.

## Disputes And Review

Creators may contact support for payout questions where payouts are active. Fraud, copyright, chargeback, tax, or provider holds may require manual review and supporting documentation.
