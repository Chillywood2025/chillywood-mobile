# Content Takedown Decisions

Emergency controls, incident response, and kill-switch governance is documented in `docs/ops/EMERGENCY_CONTROLS_INCIDENT_RESPONSE_KILL_SWITCH_GOVERNANCE.md`. Emergency moderation/takedown actions remain exact-scope, reasoned, audited where backed, and evidence-preserving; emergency disable is not hard delete, refund, payout movement, or provider mutation.

Content takedown decisions: Closed for production decision policy and current backed enforcement after validation. Live-room moderation and incident response: Closed for production policy, current backed host controls, LiveKit token authority, and incident-response proof after validation. Chat/call moderation and notification abuse controls: Closed after validation. Dedicated chat_thread report target: Closed after validation. Chat-message hide/remove/restore: Closed after validation. Account restriction and appeals operations: Closed for current production policy and existing backed enforcement. Direct mutation coverage remains intentionally limited to backed target types; unsupported target types use review, escalation, support, DMCA/legal, live-ops, or future exact backend lanes rather than unsafe generic mutation.

Account restriction does not erase content/evidence, does not execute provider refunds, and does not move money. Restricted-user public Profile/Platform visibility follows scheduled-deletion, purge/de-identification, or separate takedown policy instead of report-triggered auto-hiding.

Audit log integrity and privileged action evidence governance: Closed for current repo-side takedown evidence governance. Takedowns and restores require exact scope, reason, case/report context where applicable, and audit; audit logs include before/after where practical and avoid private evidence, raw provider payloads, tokens, signed URLs, raw IPs, and secrets.

This document defines who can hide, remove, restore, quarantine, restrict playback, force-end, mark unavailable, or preserve evidence for the app's major content types. It does not create a new staff role, activate money, execute refunds, move payouts, alter provider products, weaken reporting privacy, or weaken staff hierarchy protections.

Reports do not auto-delete content. Reports create review cases. Takedowns require exact scope, reason, case/report context where applicable, and audit. Hide/quarantine/restrict is preferred over hard delete. Evidence is preserved for moderation, DMCA/legal, security, payment/access disputes, and appeals.

## Staff Authority Matrix

| Actor | Can hide/remove/restore? | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First Owner | Yes, for highest-risk owner-controlled actions and authority gates | First Owner authority remains above all staff roles and is not weakened by takedown policy |
| Owner | Yes, for scoped platform/content/legal/live decisions | Owner/First Owner controls staff scopes and activation gates |
| Admin / internal `operator` | Yes only with exact scopes such as `content_moderation`, `admin.content.hide`, `admin.content.remove`, `admin.content.restore`, `admin.comment.moderate`, `admin.room.moderate`, or `admin.live.force_end` | Admin cannot alter Owner/First Owner authority, enable money/provider/payout systems, or execute provider refunds |
| Moderator | Yes only with exact moderation/support/live scopes | Moderator cannot grant/revoke Owner or Admin/operator, alter First Owner succession, enable money/provider/payout systems, or execute provider refunds |
| Creator | Can unpublish/delete own eligible content through normal product paths where allowed | Creator cannot erase moderation/legal/payment evidence or override staff/DMCA/security actions |
| User | Can delete own eligible content through normal product paths where allowed | User cannot erase moderation/legal/payment evidence or reporter/case records |

## Backend Enforcement Model

Takedown action target type must be allowlisted. Action type must be allowlisted: hide, unhide, restore, remove, quarantine, restrict_playback, force_end, mark_unavailable, escalate, or close_no_violation. Current generic report target mutation is intentionally narrower and supports hide/remove/restore for backed target types only.

Actor must have exact scope. Moderator/Admin action requires an active staff role and exact permission. Creator/user self-actions apply only to own eligible content and cannot erase evidence. Reason is required for staff destructive/sensitive actions. Case/report ID is required where action comes from a report/moderation case. Before/after state should be recorded where practical. Audit record must be written. Evidence preservation must be documented or recorded. Paid-access impact must be recorded but not refunded automatically. Provider refunds must not execute. Payouts/money movement must not execute. Failure errors must be sanitized. Non-staff/unscoped attempts must be denied. First Owner protected content/identity cannot be altered by Admin/Moderator through unsafe paths.

## Content Availability Behavior

Hidden, quarantined, admin-removed, scan-pending, malware-blocked, restricted, unavailable, canceled, stale, or ended content must not appear in public feed surfaces. Direct access shows safe unavailable copy rather than raw internal reasons, raw IDs, raw backend errors, raw storage paths, signed URLs, tokens, provider IDs, raw IPs, secrets, tax IDs, or bank details. Owner/creator/staff may see status badges only where allowed. Paid buyers see access-unavailable copy plus support path. Evidence remains available only to scoped staff in case/report context. Draft/private/Circle-only protections remain intact.

## Evidence Preservation Model

Evidence is preserved for moderation, DMCA/legal, security, payment/access disputes, and appeals. Report target actions preserve the report row, target before/after state, resolution reason, actor, timestamp, and audit rows. DMCA/legal evidence uses case-scoped legal evidence paths and preserve-evidence actions. Hard delete is not expanded by this policy and remains limited to existing owner-controlled account purge/de-identification/legal policy after preservation obligations are satisfied.

## Paid Content / Access / Refund Support Model

Paid-access history is preserved. Takedown does not execute provider refunds. Takedown does not enable payouts or move money. Takedown does not create payable balances. Takedown does not enable creator-money switches. Takedown does not enable Premium public activation.

When paid videos, VIP/subscriber content, paid event access, event passes, future creator paid-access unlocks, or saved replays become unavailable because of safety/legal/support policy, users see safe unavailable copy and support can review access/refund status. Manual/external refund/access support path is documented in `docs/support/MONEY_SUPPORT_WORKFLOW.md`. Staff may record manual/external refund support status only with exact scope; no Google Play, RevenueCat, Stripe, payout, cash-out, transfer, or provider refund is executed by takedown.

## UI / Command Center Model

The Owner/Admin Command Center Reports tab exposes report-linked target actions only where backed, scoped, reasoned, and audited. Manual standalone target mutation remains disabled because immutable report-linked audit requires a `safety_reports` row. Unsupported targets show honest unavailable copy and can still be marked reviewed, dismissed, escalated, or routed to support/legal/live/security lanes. Dangerous actions are separated, require confirmation where UI-backed, and use sanitized success/failure copy. No fake buttons should appear active.

## Notification / Appeal Model

Reported users are not notified merely because a report was filed. Content owners may be notified when moderation action is taken, with safe copy. High-risk, legal, security, or live-safety cases may delay notification where policy requires investigation. Notification copy must not reveal reporter identity. Appeals use support/escalation workflow in V1 unless a full appeal UI exists. Appeal handling does not expose reporter identity and must be audited.

## Launch Status

Content takedown decisions are production-ready as a scoped decision system for the current app-controlled launch mode after validation. Direct mutation coverage remains intentionally limited to backed content targets; unsupported target types require escalation, support/legal/live ops, or a future exact backend lane. This policy does not activate money/provider/payout systems and does not change staff hierarchy.

No reporter identity, raw storage paths, signed URLs, raw IPs, tokens, provider secrets, tax IDs, bank details, or private provider IDs are exposed.
