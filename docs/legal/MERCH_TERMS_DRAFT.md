# Merch Terms Draft

Status: draft only. Not legal advice. Production merch is not active. Legal, tax, fulfillment, refund/return, support, fraud, Data Safety, and Owner/Admin review are required before production merch launch.

## Physical Goods Only

Merch means physical goods only. Merch checkout must not unlock Premium, paid content, tickets, access passes, seat passes, event passes, tips, RevenueCat entitlements, digital access grants, LiveKit publish, host/speaker/mod/admin authority, payout access, or creator payable balances.

## Product Listings

Future merch listings must include accurate product descriptions, images, sizing, pricing, availability, shipping timing, return limitations, and support instructions. Inventory and fulfillment status must not be faked.

## Pricing And Payment

Stripe may be used for production physical merch only after live approval. Android digital goods must not route to Stripe merch checkout.

## Shipping And Fulfillment

Shipping timing, fulfillment partner/manual workflow, tracking, delayed shipment notices, damaged/missing item handling, and out-of-stock cancellation rules must be ready before production.

## Returns, Exchanges, Refunds

Return window, exchange policy, non-returnable item categories, damaged item process, missing item process, wrong item process, and full refund when an order cannot be fulfilled require legal/support approval before production.

## Support

Support must collect order id, product, shipping/contact context, fulfillment state, tracking state, photos for damaged items where appropriate, and customer communication history. Support must not guarantee delivery or refunds without policy and fulfillment proof.
