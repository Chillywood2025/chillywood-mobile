# Merch Fulfillment Plan

Status: draft operations plan. Production merch checkout is not active.

## Model

Launch planning assumes manual fulfillment first. Print-on-demand or third-party fulfillment can be reviewed later if chosen.

## Product Operations

- SKU and product management;
- inventory status;
- product image/description approval;
- pricing and tax review;
- shipping time promises;
- customer address handling and privacy retention;
- fulfillment owner and escalation path.

## Order States

- `pending`
- `paid`
- `processing`
- `shipped`
- `delivered`
- `canceled`
- `refunded`
- `disputed`
- `failed`
- `blocked`

## Fulfillment States

- `not_started`
- `processing`
- `shipped`
- `delivered`
- `canceled`
- `returned`
- `blocked`

## Required Before Production

Shipping policy, refund/return policy, damaged/missing item support, tracking strategy, fraud checks, support workflow, tax review, Data Safety review, and Owner/Admin activation approval.
