# Merch Operation Checklist

Status: draft checklist. Production merch is not active.

Before enabling production merch:

- approve physical-goods-only product catalog;
- verify Stripe live physical merch checkout server-side;
- verify signed live webhook endpoint;
- confirm no digital access/RevenueCat/Premium entitlement from merch;
- complete fulfillment owner/process;
- complete shipping timing policy;
- complete tracking number strategy;
- complete customer address retention policy;
- complete refund/return support playbook;
- complete sales tax/VAT/GST review;
- complete fraud/order review process;
- complete support queue and escalation ownership;
- complete Data Safety/privacy review;
- attach Owner/Admin approval and rollback plan.
