# Creator Payout Support Playbook

Date: 2026-06-30

This playbook covers cashout readiness only. It is not a payout execution runbook.

## Current State

Creators can access cashout readiness, but real cashout is not live. Cashout readiness does not execute payouts. No real payout, transfer, withdrawal, or payable balance is created. `liveMoneyEnabled` remains OFF. Payouts and cashout remain OFF for production money movement.

## Support Response

When a creator asks about cashout today:

1. Confirm that payout setup/readiness can be reviewed in the app.
2. Explain that cashout is not live yet.
3. Explain that no real payout will be sent from readiness review.
4. Explain that production cashout requires Stripe/live provider approval, tax/KYC readiness, fraud/support/legal review, and owner approval.
5. Do not promise withdrawal timing, payable balances, transfers, or payout release.
6. Record the support note without banking, tax, provider, private email, phone, token, or raw auth data.

## Not Allowed

- No payout execution.
- No cashout execution.
- No withdrawal or transfer.
- No payable balance creation.
- No payout promise.
- No provider mutation.
- No live money activation.
