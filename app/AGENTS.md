# App assurance boundary

Before app work, select the exact feature manifest and enumerate routes,
components, session/auth states, native modules, providers, platform scope,
failure states, accessibility, rollback, and installed golden cases.

UI or JavaScript evidence cannot prove native lifecycle, provider delivery,
signed artifact identity, installed behavior, first media, purchase/restore, or
physical interaction. Keep Android and iOS claims separate. Responsive Layout
V2 and AppLovin are out of scope until their registered prerequisites pass.
