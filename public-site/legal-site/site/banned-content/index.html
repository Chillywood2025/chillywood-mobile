Chi'llywood Public Policy

Banned Content

Content and behavior Chi'llywood does not allow.

Last updated: May 13, 2026

The following content and behavior are banned on Chi'llywood.

Child Safety

  • child sexual abuse material;
  • sexualization of minors;
  • grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or exploitation of minors;
  • attempts to solicit minors for sexual content or private contact;
  • any content that endangers minors.

Non-Consensual Sexual Content

  • non-consensual intimate content;
  • revenge porn;
  • deepfake sexual abuse;
  • coerced sexual content;
  • sexual exploitation or trafficking;
  • threats to share intimate content.

Violence And Threats

  • credible threats;
  • incitement to violence;
  • instructions for serious harm;
  • terrorist or extremist recruitment;
  • glorification of violent attacks;
  • unsafe live behavior likely to cause immediate harm.

Hate And Harassment

  • protected-class attacks;
  • dehumanization;
  • violent hate;
  • slurs used to attack people;
  • stalking;
  • doxxing;
  • coordinated harassment.

Privacy Violations

  • private addresses, phone numbers, financial data, credentials, private images, private messages, or sensitive personal information shared without permission.

Illegal Or Regulated Goods

  • illegal goods or services;
  • illegal weapons sales;
  • illegal drugs;
  • illegal gambling;
  • regulated goods where disallowed or unlawfully promoted.

Fraud, Scams, And Platform Abuse

  • phishing;
  • malware;
  • harmful files;
  • credential theft;
  • fake giveaways;
  • fake Premium offers;
  • fake sponsors;
  • fake followers, fake views, fake engagement;
  • bots, spam, scraping, and coordinated manipulation.

Self-Harm And Dangerous Acts

  • encouragement of self-harm or suicide;
  • dangerous challenges;
  • instructions for serious self-injury;
  • exploitation of vulnerable people.

Graphic Violence And Cruelty

Graphic violence, gore, torture, animal cruelty, or shocking injury content may be removed or restricted, especially when posted to celebrate, encourage, exploit, or sensationalize harm.

Intellectual Property And Impersonation

  • copyright infringement;
  • trademark infringement;
  • stolen creator uploads;
  • pirated movies, shows, music, clips, or broadcasts;
  • impersonation;
  • fake official status;
  • counterfeit promotion.

Evasion

Do not evade account bans, moderation decisions, copyright actions, Premium gates, upload limits, live restrictions, fraud holds, sponsor restrictions, or safety systems.

Immediate Removal / Escalation

Severe violations may be removed immediately and may lead to account termination, preservation of evidence, provider escalation, rights-holder escalation, or law-enforcement/legal escalation where required.
